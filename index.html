Style guide: choosing the right dress 📚

A short read for anyone who wants to dress with confidence.

1. Start with comfort 🌼

A dress looks best when you feel at ease in it. Check that you can sit, walk and raise your arms comfortably. Confidence shows more than any trend.

2. Know your silhouettes ✂️

Silhouette means the overall shape of a dress. The table below is a simple starting point, not a rule. Try different shapes and keep what makes you happy.

SilhouetteWhat it looks likeGreat for
A-lineFitted top, skirt flares gentlyEveryday, college, brunch
WrapCrosses at the waist, tie closureWork, dinner, travel
Slip / ColumnStraight, skims the bodyEvenings, layering
Fit & flareDefined waist, full skirtParties, festivals
MaxiLong, flowing to the ankleHolidays, summer days
Small changes create big impacts.

3. Accessories do the magic 💍

The same dress can feel casual with sneakers and a tote, or dressy with earrings and heels. A belt, scarf or jacket changes the mood without buying anything new.

4. Choose fabrics for the season 🧵

Cotton and linen keep you cool in summer. Satin and crepe drape elegantly for evenings. Wool blends and knits add warmth in winter. Always check the care label before washing.

Two or three neutral dresses in cream, black or denim blue.

2. Add colour

One or two shades you love, such as rose or sage.

3. Layer smartly

A jacket, cardigan and scarf create many looks from few dresses.
